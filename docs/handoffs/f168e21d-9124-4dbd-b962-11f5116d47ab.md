**Trap for anyone repeating this:** an unscoped `filter-repo` rewrites **177 of 208** commits, back
to the repo's second day, because `fast-export` strips GPG signatures and 47 merge commits are
GitHub-signed — changing those cascades to every descendant. Always scope with `--refs <range>` and
diff the old/new SHA sets *before* pushing.
